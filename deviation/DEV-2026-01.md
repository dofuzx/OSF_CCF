## **Summary of Deviation**

Content Locked at Registration is For Step B1, the sensitivity analysis method was specified as ANCOM-BC2, with a direction consistency rate of ≥80% as the decision threshold.

Deviation in the sensitivity analysis method: ANCOM-BC2 → CLR-limma + permutation test.

## Details of the Deviation

ANCOM-BC2 could not be compiled in the execution environment (the dependency chain CVXR → clarabel requires a Rust build, and rust-lld could not locate the liblapack library in the conda toolchain). After three rounds of remediation attempts—installing rustc and GSL and setting RUSTFLAGS—all of them failed with the same linker error. The sensitivity direction consistency check was therefore replaced with the limma linear model following CLR transformation (with the same covariates) combined with a sign-consistency comparison, supplemented by 10,000-permutation testing for correction.



## Impact Assessment

Both methods are differential abundance tests for compositional data; the limma-on-CLR approach retains the covariate structure of MaAsLin 2. This deviation affects only the software implementation used for sensitivity verification; it does not alter the test targets, the samples, the threshold (FDR < 0.05), or the decision threshold (≥80% direction consistency rate).
